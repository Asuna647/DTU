1. Executive Summary

Digital Saarthi is an AI-powered "Action-Guidance Layer" designed to help elderly citizens and people with low digital literacy access government services. Unlike existing platforms like UMANG or DigiLocker that assume technical proficiency, Digital Saarthi provides:

  • Voice-first interaction in Hindi and English
  • Document scanning with automatic data extraction
  • Step-by-step guidance with visual action plans
  • Verified information through a hardcoded rule engine
  • Accessibility-first UI with large buttons, high contrast, and text-to-speech

4. Component Details

4.1 Rule Engine (app/rule_engine.py)

The Safety Core of Digital Saarthi ensures zero hallucinations by using deterministic, hardcoded eligibility logic.

Key Rule for IGNOAPS Pension:
Age ≥ 60 AND Has BPL Card = ELIGIBLE
Age < 60 OR No BPL Card = NOT ELIGIBLE

4.2 Speech Service (app/speech_service.py)

Handles voice interaction, intent detection, and text-to-speech audio generation for accessibility.

4.3 OCR Service (app/ocr_service.py)

Parses uploaded Aadhaar cards or government notices using Tesseract and regex pattern matching.
